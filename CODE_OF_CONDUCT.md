# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good intent, and focus feedback on the work rather than the person.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without permission.

## Maintainer responsibility

Project maintainers should keep discussions constructive, explain moderation decisions when practical, and prioritize a safe environment for contributors.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. Reports will be reviewed and handled based on the severity and context of the incident.
